Needs you / Codex proposal cp-0042

Raise the breeder job cap to 12 GPU-hours

your signature

Statute S-006c · proposed by legislative counsel · council 4–0

The change

 @law("S-006") @clause("c") @tier("statute") @enforcement("preventive")
-@statement("A breeder may submit a job that runs a vetted recipe for at most 8 GPU-hours; larger jobs need a per-case grant.")
+@statement("A breeder may submit a job that runs a vetted recipe for at most 12 GPU-hours; larger jobs need a per-case grant.")
 permit (principal is Agent, action == Action::"training.submit", resource)
-when { principal.role == "breeder" && context.gpu_hours <= 8 };
+when { principal.role == "breeder" && context.gpu_hours <= 12 };

Rationale

The rung predictor v3 needs about 10 GPU-hours a run. Under the 8-hour cap every run needs a grant, and last month's three grants passed unchanged. Twelve covers v3 with room to spare; larger jobs still need a grant.
