        live
        
            The council's motions: four seats vote and the count decides.
            
      
            

      
            
        council unreachable
        
            
      
            

      
            
        
            
          
            motions
            
          
            still open
            
          
            awaiting the owner
            
          
            ties · to the owner
            
        
            
        
            —
            
        
            A majority of the four seats carries a motion; a 2–2 tie escalates to the owner; a motion above the council's authority waits for the owner's ratification. An abstention counts toward the four, never the majority.
            
      
            

      
            The motions 
            
      
            
        
            
      
            
      
            
        
            No.Proposed byKindMotionStateTallyProposed
            
        
      
            
      
            
        
            Proposing and voting are seat actions, one vote per seat — a later vote replaces the earlier. The node derives a motion's state and its gate; the sheet reads them.
            
        
            The owner's decision. The decide control is offered only on a tie_to_owner or awaiting_owner motion: press to arm, then Ratify or Veto, each naming the motion, commits. Once decided the motion is ratified or vetoed and the council cannot reopen it.
